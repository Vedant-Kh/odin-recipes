Pasta Carbonara


Pasta Carbonara is a classic Italian dish made with eggs, hard cheese (typically Pecorino Romano or Parmigiano Reggiano), cured pork (traditionally guanciale, but pancetta or bacon are common substitutes), and black pepper.

Ingredients:

  • pasta
  • oil
  • veggies

Steps:

  1. Cook the pasta – Boil spaghetti or bucatini in salted water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup of starchy pasta water before draining.
  2. Render the meat – Meanwhile, cook guanciale (or pancetta/bacon) in a cold pan over medium heat until the fat renders and the pieces are crispy. Remove from heat.
